Posters @ the 37th European Society of Pathology

2025-09-10

The MetPredict team is excited to present our latest research findings at the 37th European Society of Pathology conference. This event will provide an excellent opportunity to share our work with the broader scientific community and engage in discussions about the future of metastatic potential prediction.

Yimin had an e-poster on LazySlide, our lab's framework for whole slide image analysis. He also presented a poster on our collaboration with Juliane Winkler funded by the Vienna Science and Technology foundation (WWTF) on breast cancer metastasis.

Congratulations to Yimin and thank you to the organizers for selecting our work!
